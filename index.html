About The Project

A Landmark in Luxury Living

Welcome to Eldeco 7 Peaks Residences — a landmark luxury development in Omicron 1A, Greater Noida. Spread across 7.5 acres, this ultra-low-density project features just 7 standalone towers designed for resort-style living with infinite green views.

With architecture by globally acclaimed AEDAS (Hong Kong) and landscape design by Coopers Hill (Singapore), every home is a masterpiece. All 900 premium residences are 3-side open, facing greens or central tropical water features.
